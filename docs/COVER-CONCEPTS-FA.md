> **English summary.** This document proposes cover layouts for the eight-product homepage of the storefront; a visual preview is available at `cover-previews/index.html`. The cover uses eight real products: iPhone 18 Pro Max, Galaxy S26 Ultra, ROG Strix G16, PlayStation 5 Pro, Xiaomi 18 Pro Max, Legion 7i, iPhone Air and POCO X8 Pro. Five layouts are compared, each with a colour palette: Galaxy Grid (a neat 4x2 grid, judged the most suitable), Orbit Arc (an orbit-like arc with a glowing centre), Launch Podium (a launch stage with three products in front and five behind), Constellation (a bridge layout with star-like lines) and Prism Beams (vertical columns with light beams). The selected direction combines the Constellation layout with Prism Beams, using an Ocean Blue halo and a Rose Nebula glow. It has been applied in `components/home-cover.tsx` and `enhancements.css`, replacing the earlier three-product cover. The original Persian text follows unchanged.
